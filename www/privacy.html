Privacy Policy — Udhar

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · App: Udhar — Lend & Borrow Tracker (com.deepdharia.udhar)

1. Your data stays on your device

Udhar is an offline-first personal ledger. Every entry you record — names, amounts, dates, notes — is stored only in your phone's local storage. We do not operate servers, we do not have accounts or logins, and we never transmit your ledger anywhere.

2. No data collection

Udhar does not collect, track, or share any personal or sensitive user data — no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, no location, no contacts access. The app requests zero Android permissions in its base version.

3. Backups you control

The optional Export feature writes a JSON file to a location you choose (your phone, your Drive, your email). That file is yours; we never see it.

4. WhatsApp sharing

The "nudge on WhatsApp" button simply opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message you send. No data passes through us.

5. Children's privacy

Udhar is a general-purpose finance utility not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone.

6. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL and the "Last updated" date revised.

7. Contact

Questions: Deep Dharia — via the support email listed on the Google Play store page.
