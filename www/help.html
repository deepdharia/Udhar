Understand your balance

GAVE records money going out. GOT BACK records money coming in. A positive balance means that person owes you; a negative balance means you owe them. All shared balances use the sender’s perspective.

Keep a backup

Open Settings → Backup data. On Android, copy the JSON into a safe place; on the web, keep the downloaded file. Backups are unencrypted. The ledger stays on this device, and clearing storage or uninstalling can delete it. A browser or device may also clear local storage.

To restore, open Settings → Restore backup and choose a JSON file or paste its text. Check the displayed counts before confirming replacement. Export your current ledger first.

Testing Udhar?

Use sample names and amounts. Try the following on your own phone; record anything confusing or incorrect.

  1. Add “Test friend” with GAVE 500. Add GOT BACK 200 for the same name. The balance should be 300 to receive.
  2. Edit the 200 repayment to 250. The balance should become 250. Test deleting an entry, including cancelling the deletion.
  3. Tap Settle up, check the amount, and confirm using the matching payment button. The balance should become zero.
  4. Close and reopen the app; check that the entries remain. Repeat an entry with airplane mode enabled.
  5. Back up your sample ledger. Add another sample entry, then restore the backup; confirm that the restored entries match.
  6. Share only a sample ledger. Open the link on another device and check the names, amounts, and read-only snapshot.
  7. Try Settings, privacy, Back, the keyboard, and screen rotation. Report any hidden controls or crashes.

Send useful feedback

Include the app version, phone model, Android version, steps you took, what you expected, and what happened. Retest an issue after an update and say whether it is fixed.

Keep financial details private. Never post real names, ledger contents, backup JSON, or share links in a public issue. Use sample data in screenshots.

Closed testers can leave private feedback on the Google Play testing listing while signed into their enrolled account. You can also use the same private channel through which you joined the test.

For non-sensitive technical issues, open a public GitHub issue. This link opens a form; nothing is submitted automatically.
